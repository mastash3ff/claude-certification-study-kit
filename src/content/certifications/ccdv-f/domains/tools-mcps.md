Tool schemas, names, descriptions, error categories, side effects, approvals, and result contracts determine reliable use. Choose MCP when an independently maintained capability must be reusable across clients; choose Skills for reusable guidance and hooks for deterministic event controls.

Practice redesigning two overlapping tools until selection and recovery are unambiguous.
