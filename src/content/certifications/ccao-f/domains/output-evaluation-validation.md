Evaluate output against the actual decision, not its fluency. Separate factual correctness from completeness, reasoning quality, audience fit, and formatting. Verify material claims against authoritative sources and escalate high-impact judgments to accountable people.

Practice annotating a Claude draft with claims, missing evidence, potential bias, audience changes, and the exact verification owner.
