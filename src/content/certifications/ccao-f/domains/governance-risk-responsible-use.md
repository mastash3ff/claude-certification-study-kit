Classify the data, decision impact, affected people, applicable policy, and accountable owner before using Claude. Prompts cannot replace authorization, privacy controls, required human review, or organizational approval.

Practice distinguishing a low-risk draft from a regulated or consequential decision and naming the additional controls.
