Choose the simplest product surface and least costly model that meets measured quality, latency, and workflow needs. Projects fit maintained instructions and knowledge; research fits open-ended evidence gathering; Artifacts fit outputs users need to inspect or reuse.

Practice routing five recurring tasks and recording why a more complex surface or model would be wasteful.
