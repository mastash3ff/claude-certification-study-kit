title: Prompting and Task Execution
description: Brief Claude clearly, decompose complex work, and iterate by task type.
order: 10
---

Treat a prompt as a professional brief: define the goal, audience, source material, constraints, and output format. For complex work, separate evidence gathering, analysis, checking, and presentation into visible stages.

Practice by drafting one business communication, one source-based analysis, and one brainstorming request. For each, explain why the prompt structure changes with the task.
