## Study at the associate level

This track tests whether you can turn a business task into a clear Claude interaction, judge the result, and know when the work needs stronger evidence or accountable human review. It does not require API development.

Start with the diagnostic before reading the domain pages. Repair weak objectives with the official guide and Claude Help Center, then practice the behavior in a real but non-sensitive workflow.

## Recurring decisions

- Give Claude the task, context, audience, constraints, and output format a capable colleague would need.
- Treat fluent output as a draft until its accuracy, completeness, audience fit, and risk have been checked.
- Choose the least complex Claude product and model that meets the task.
- Keep Projects, instructions, connectors, and knowledge sources current and owned.
- Follow organizational policy before convenience, especially for sensitive or regulated data.
- Diagnose the failing layer before changing prompts, products, or workflow steps.

## Readiness standard

You are ready when you can explain why an approach fits the audience, evidence, workflow, risk, cost, and quality constraint, not merely recognize a feature name.
