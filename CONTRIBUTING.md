# Contributing study content

This is a local study repository, but the content contract is explicit so future additions remain trustworthy.

- Map every question to one current official objective.
- Write original scenarios and answer choices. Do not submit anything remembered, reconstructed, photographed, or copied from a live exam.
- Give every option a specific rationale explaining why it is best or worse under the stated constraint.
- Cite at least one primary source. Candidate reports may support study technique, never the correct answer.
- Label unofficial formats, inferred behavior, and dated product details.
- Keep raw practice percentages separate from Anthropic's scaled score.
- Update the verification date when reviewing a new Exam Guide version.

Run `python3 scripts/check.py` before committing.
