A diagnostic-first workflow

Turn weak objectives into a study plan.

Take the local diagnostic cold, study the highest-priority gaps, build the labs, and only then use an external mock for unseen calibration.

Scores in this kit are raw percentages, not Anthropic scaled scores or pass predictions.

Recommended sequence

  1. Choose Foundations or Professional and finish the full diagnostic.
  2. Review missed answers and follow their primary-source links.
  3. Work through the matching guide and hands-on exercises in this repository's docs/ directory.
  4. Retake weak areas with domain drills.
  5. Use the external mock linked from the results page as final calibration.

Source order

Trust the official exam guide for scope, Anthropic documentation for product behavior, and community material for explanation and practice. Verify eligibility and scheduling details in the Anthropic certification FAQ.
