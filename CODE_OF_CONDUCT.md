# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be useful, respectful, and free from harassment. Be constructive, assume good faith while checking claims, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project discussion.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
